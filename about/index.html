About NuIQ

HIPAA-compliant AI that writes your therapy notes automatically.

At NuIQ, our journey began with a simple yet profound idea rooted in solving billion-dollar problems using technology. Most of these problems lie in the bottom two levels of Maslow’s Hierarchy of Needs. As we explored each level, we discovered a unique opportunity in the powerful space of mental health. Recognizing the significance of this realm and its impact on overall well-being, we embarked on an adventure to bring advancing technology to the forefront.

Our Inspiration: Maslow’s Pyramid

Maslow’s Hierarchy of Needs served as our guiding beacon. We saw an untapped potential to revolutionize the mental health space through innovative solutions. Starting with the foundational need for psychological well-being, we envisioned a tool that would empower mental health professionals and elevate their practice.

Our Mission

Transforming mental health practices by simplifying therapists’ lives, providing innovative solutions, saving time and enabling them to focus on what truly matters: their clients.

Our Vision

Empowering clinicians nationwide with cutting-edge technology to elevate their professional and personal lives, fostering a community of professionals dedicated to advancing the field of behavioral health.
